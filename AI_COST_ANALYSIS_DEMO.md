### 1. Citizen Posts Problem
- Takes photo of pothole/water leak/electrical issue

- **AI automatically analyzes the photo**

### 2. AI Cost Analysis
- Groq AI examines the problem image
- Considers category, description, location
- **Generates detailed cost breakdown**

### 3. Government Dashboard
- Officers see **estimated repair costs**
- View detailed analysis with materials needed
- Make informed budget decisions
